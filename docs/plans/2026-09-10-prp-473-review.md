## Findings

### P1 / High - Non-SMS provenance is lost at planned-tour boundaries

Changed location: `src/lib/tour-notification-delivery.server.ts:525`. The new compatibility rule permits conversation evidence whenever `smsOrigin` is absent. `createTourInquiry` correctly stamps new voice/email/web requests `non_sms`, but `confirmTourInquiry` at `src/lib/tour-inquiry-confirm.server.ts:279` builds a planned event without that field. `inquiryFromPlannedEvent` at `src/lib/tour-planned-change.server.ts:47` also drops it. Therefore a new non-SMS request with unchecked consent can borrow an old SMS conversation grant after confirmation, even though the same inquiry was blocked while pending.

Preserve server-owned origin through both projections. Cover actual create/confirm/planned-reschedule and cancel paths with the real eligibility resolver. Tool-argument assertions cannot establish this contract.

### P1 / High - Derived conversation authority is not consistently revoked

Changed location: `src/lib/sms/tour-sms-eligibility.server.ts:120`, and materialization at line 163. The resolver copies `twilio_start` from a conversation into a tour-purpose grant, but only rechecks the conversation for `recipient_initiated_inbound`. Both independent reviewers executed the actual transpiled resolver against a scoped ledger and reproduced `eligible:true` after a newer conversation revoke when the copied source is `twilio_start`; the ordinary inbound source correctly failed. Missing/unreadable conversation evidence has the same bypass.

The dependency also disappears at delayed dispatch: `src/lib/sms/owner-sms-dispatcher.server.ts:466` calls `loadSendPolicy`, whose consent check at line 149 delegates to `ensureApplicationScopedSmsConsent`. Its granted-purpose early return (`src/lib/sms/application-consent.server.ts:28`) never revalidates the source conversation. A revoke after enqueue therefore leaves a derived lifecycle grant usable. This delayed-dispatch interleaving is source-confirmed, not a real provider send.

Global STOP and exact-purpose revocation still block; this finding specifically concerns conversation-derived authority. Preserve derivation evidence and validate it for START and ordinary inbound sources both before enqueue and at provider dispatch. Distinguish independently restored purpose grants from grants derived from a START-restored conversation.
