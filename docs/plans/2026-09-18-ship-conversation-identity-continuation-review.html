R20 - P2: application submission records a role that contradicts its verified prospect binding

src/lib/application-submitted-notification.server.ts:143-149 deliberately accepts an existing prospect or applicant SMS conversation, but :173 always stamps the email identity as counterpartyRole: "applicant". The native history retains its durable prospect role; src/lib/manager-sms-messages.server.ts:654-669 updates directory labels and tenancy status without replacing that role. The new manager email and SMS keys include their respective roles (src/components/portal/pro-unified-inbox.tsx:163-190; src/lib/portal-inbox-storage.ts:432).

Concrete case: a property-A prospect history K exists, then that person submits an application for A. The submission's email key ends in the applicant relationship while K's native key ends in the prospect relationship. They remain separate list rows even with an exact owner, property and binding. A prior prospect email notice is also incompatible with the new applicant claim under the storage partition. This breaks the primary prospect-to-application continuity requirement.

Correction: when exact lookup proves an existing native key, retain its immutable role as binding provenance; keep current applicant/resident status separate. Apply the same contract in compatibility and rendering. Do not broadly equate all prospect/applicant/resident keys or weaken owner/property isolation.

Required regression: exercise the real submitted-notification producer with a matched prospect history, then consume its written email row with the manager inbox/native payload. Assert one row, complete tour/prospect/application history, stable K, and correct applicant label. Cover a fresh applicant-only conversation and different-property/owner/role negative cases.

R21 - P2: opening an isolated manager email row bypasses the new property check

The list now rejects incompatible SMS relationships, but the selected pane still admits them. src/components/portal/pro-unified-inbox.tsx:971-974 enables direct chat for any selected email row with declared SMS bindings, even if that row was intentionally isolated. At :1238-1251, selectedSmsResidents then loads SMS by those bindings alone, without checking the relationship or whether that native row was accepted into the selected row's members. The resulting SMS history is passed to the pane at :1318-1319 and included in read marking at :1262-1264.

Concrete case: property-B email declares K, while native K is stamped property A or has multiple property tags. The list helper correctly keeps them separate. Opening B nevertheless displays K's history and can mark its inbound turns read. A legacy conflicted email row likewise keeps its scalar binding and reaches this path. This is a deterministic selection/timeline mismatch; this report does not claim the server returned otherwise unauthorized SMS to the viewer.

Correction: use the same verified relationship decision for list membership, selected timeline, reply target and read side effects. A declared binding is necessary provenance but must not bypass a rejected property relationship. Keep rejected histories separately reachable.

Required regression: mount the actual manager inbox, click the property-B row with K, and assert the A/multi-property SMS body is absent and its ids are not acknowledged. Repeat with conflicting stored provenance. A positive compatible A/K case must render both channels and acknowledge only its members. Pure key-helper tests are insufficient; the existing two helper tests never open a row.

R22 - P2: unknown historical content gains a property before the resident isolation guard runs

src/lib/portal-inbox-storage.ts:403-405 accepts an empty claim set, and :435-448 similarly admits an unknown same-email row into the first compatible collapse partition. The collapse then copies the sole known property/manager onto the combined history at :1321-1323 and :1350-1356. Resident GET performs that collapse before rendering (src/app/api/portal-inbox-threads/route.ts:120-124). Consequently the later resident guard cannot recognize the unknown source: it receives one apparently proven row with both histories.

The write path does the same permanently: a newer generic same-email row passes compatible lookup at src/lib/portal-inbox-delivery.ts:403-407, then :92-95 fills its missing property from the incoming notification. With histories A, B and an unstamped historical row U, U joins whichever compatible partition is encountered first; with only A present, U still becomes A without supporting evidence. This violates the explicit continuation instruction to keep unknown historical evidence conservative instead of stamping old content with a new property. It is a demonstrated identity/provenance defect; no production disclosure was reproduced.

Correction: distinguish absence of contradiction from positive proof of relationship. Preserve unknown source content separately unless an authorized durable reference proves it belongs to the relationship. Apply this at both persisted row reuse and read collapse, so source ids, read/action membership and historical content are not reclassified by a later notice. Preserve already-proven compatible generic histories where real evidence exists.

Required regression: pass U/A/B through the real storage collapse and resident GET-to-component path, in multiple input orders, and assert U stays independently selectable with its own action/read membership. Deliver A into a store whose newest same-email row is unknown and verify U is unchanged and the A write/retry is isolated. Include one positive legacy row whose relationship is actually proven. The resident component's unknown-row fixture alone bypasses the upstream collapse and cannot prove this contract.
