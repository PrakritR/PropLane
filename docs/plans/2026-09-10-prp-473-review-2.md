The security delegate independently executed the actual reply module with mocked storage. The general recorder's same-operation check fails, so the terminal guard is bypassed and its new-proposal branch overwrites B with awaiting A (`src/lib/tour-reschedule-sms-reply.server.ts:144`, `:171`). The recursive handler confirms A because planned candidate matching (`:291`) and the final reply CAS (`:424`) do not compare proposal generation to the row's current `rescheduleNotificationGeneration`.

Probe exit 0: contested read occurred; two writes; final row generation B, proposal generation A, proposal status confirmed. The initial repair call at `:328` supplies no expected snapshot. This violates the explicit upgrade-only and never-reopen requirements, independently of consent availability or transport.

Require an upgrade-only CAS bound to the original event/proposal and current row generation. A changed generation, status, sender, owner, phone, window, or provenance must reject instead of falling through to creation.
