Evidence and decision

Root reports that supervised attempt v3 failed at own-backend-close / zero-inventory, with requested=true, response-kind=query-rejection, error-code=unknown, event-source=observer and inventory=different. Process group 43162 fully exited; there was no watchdog timeout, lease-renewal failure or publication. Source inspection shows the accepted shutdown path reached inventory before closing the logical observer client. This supports a replacement-backend hypothesis, not proof of provider causality or ownership of the different row.

Root's subsequent read-only observation is especially relevant: v3 ended at 18:22:37; the different backend began at 18:22:37.135, remained idle at 18:23:47.564, and complete inventory was zero at 18:24:46.747. Root reports no observer connection or termination between those observations; evidence is retained in observer-after-passive-drain.json. This independently fits a roughly two-minute passive drain, without proving the exact expiry instant or deployed provider version. This planning context did not access that private evidence file.

Root also reports the original normal-close backend remained visible from 17:55:33 through a 17:57 read-only observation and later disappeared. Public Supavisor source provides a plausible timing explanation: its manager uses a 120,000 ms subscriber-check timer, resets it on a client DOWN event, and shuts down the pool when no subscribers remain. This supports a passive wait exceeding two minutes. It does not establish the deployed provider version or guarantee this project's exact expiry time. Supavisor Manager source, lines 19, 416-475.

Supavisor documents pool-mode database workers as long-lived and shared across clients. Consequently, closing a client alone is not physical-backend disappearance. Merely moving client closure ahead of the current ten-second poll could still fail on normal pool retention; retaining self-termination also creates needless replacement risk. The selected correction relies exclusively on fresh inventory to establish disappearance. Supavisor DbHandler source, lines 8-16.

Supervision amendment

The prior 120-second watchdog cannot accommodate the documented two-minute idle drain plus proof and publication. Root must obtain independent source review of the new helper and the explicit 270-second total wall-clock supervisor before any retry. The 150-second wait includes a 30-second margin over the public source's timer; the remaining outer budget accommodates setup, proof and final gates but guarantees no successful completion time. Slow operations may still fail closed at the outer deadline.

Root launches the exact pinned CLI in its own verified process group. A separate process renews exclusion every eight seconds using unchanged maximum 60-second receipt lifetime/age, binding the new recovery digest. Validate receipts during the drain; do not merely trust the renewal process. Keep all local, scheduled and CI consumers excluded. No longer-lived receipt, relaxed final 35-second gate or watchdog reset is permitted.

At 270 seconds the independent supervisor stops that exact group, escalates to SIGKILL after a short bounded grace and verifies all descendants exited, including GitHub and Supabase children. Retain exclusion and evidence through diagnosis. This remains essential for the frozen connector's pre-return stall gap. Timeout or lost publication response is failure/ambiguity; a later attempt may use only the same retained bytes after full fresh proof. No automated retry, publication, credential mutation or evidence deletion by the watchdog.
