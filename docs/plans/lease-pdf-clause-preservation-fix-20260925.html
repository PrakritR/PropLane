Result: The numbered clauses that disappeared in the initial browser QA now appear in the converted preview, in source order with their original numbers.

Cause and change

The PDF parser put short numbered clauses wholly in section titles. buildProplaneLeaseHtmlFromSections then discarded every section without body text, losing clauses 1 through 3. It also prefixed its own ordinal to a source heading, producing “5. 4.”. The renderer now keeps a nonempty title even when body text is empty and preserves source numbering in source-only converted HTML.

Changed src/lib/lease-pdf-parse.ts and added a focused regression in tests/unit/lease-pdf-parse.test.ts.
