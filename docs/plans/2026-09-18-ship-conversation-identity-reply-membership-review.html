R25 - P2: replying to an unknown or conflicted selected source can still append to known property B

src/app/api/portal/send-inbox-message/route.ts:111-122 returns no identity for a source with absent or conflicting provenance. The authorized selected source is nevertheless committed at :576, then its counterpart is delivered with the undefined identity at :859-875. src/lib/portal-inbox-storage.ts:431-433 accepts every stored identity when the incoming identity is empty, and src/lib/portal-inbox-delivery.ts:378-407 chooses the newest same-person candidate.

Source-traced reproduction: manager opens retained legacy unknown U (or a source with conflicting A/B provenance); the resident has newer known B plus separate unknown history with that manager. Reply through the selected source. Authorization passes, U receives the manager turn, but the resident counterpart appends to B. The recipient row retains B metadata, incorrectly classifying the reply. The correction avoids inventing an identity object but still lets absence behave as a wildcard during delivery.

Correction: distinguish true fresh compose from a reply whose selected relationship is unknown or conflicted. Do not reuse a known counterpart through undefined identity. Preserve unknown history separately using a bounded safe lookup/create contract, or reject before any write when the counterpart cannot be resolved safely. Keep genuine fresh compose behavior intentional. Add route/delivery fixtures for selected unknown and conflicted histories alongside newer known A/B, asserting no cross-partition write, including retry/failed resolution.

R26 - P2: one matching recipient allows unrelated recipients to inherit the selected source identity

src/app/api/portal/send-inbox-message/route.ts:562-567 checks recipients.some(...). The entire allowed recipient array is later delivered at :802-875 using the same selected-source identity.

Source-traced reproduction: submit selected resident-A source plus recipients A and B, where both are authorized manager contacts. A satisfies the new check. B also receives a counterpart carrying A's property/manager relationship; sender-copy suppression applies to B as well because the selected source belongs to the manager. This does not demonstrate an out-of-scope recipient authorization bypass; it demonstrates incorrect relationship stamping and missing B sender history within allowed contacts. A plus broadcast recipients can take the same path.

Correction: require a selected person-thread reply to resolve to exactly its single counterparty, or independently resolve each additional recipient as a fresh conversation without copying selected-source metadata. Prefer rejecting mixed reply/broadcast payloads before commit. Extend the refusal test with A plus B and A plus broadcast, not only B alone, and assert zero writes on refusal.
