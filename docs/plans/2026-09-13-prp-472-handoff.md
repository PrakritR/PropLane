## Remaining limits

The design intentionally keeps SMS opened state device-local. A permission change racing after route preflight is not serialized across the whole batch; each source update remains atomic and the response reconciles each result. A content edit can conservatively defer a read acknowledgement until normal refresh and reopen. No scroll-position receipt model or identity redesign was added.
