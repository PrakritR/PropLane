## Description

Found in a portal audit, 2026-09-04. Four missing capabilities that together make the vendor portal feel unfinished. Grouped because they are one theme: **a vendor can receive things but cannot act on them.**

## 1. A vendor working for two managers can never submit an invoice (blocking)

`src/lib/vendor-invoice-submit.server.ts:77-83` throws `multiple_managers` (409) when `managerUserId` is absent and the vendor has more than one linked manager. `vendor-finances-panel.tsx:326` POSTs to `/api/vendor/invoices` and **never sends** `managerUserId` — there is no manager picker in the UI.

So any vendor serving two PropLane managers is **permanently blocked** from invoicing, with an error that reads like a bug: *"supports one linked manager for now"*. Serving several clients is the normal condition for a contractor; this is the base case, not an edge case.

**Fix:** add the manager picker the API already expects. Default to the work order's manager when invoicing against one.

## 2. A vendor cannot decline an offer or withdraw a bid

`work_order_vendor_offers.status` is only `"sent" | "withdrawn"` (`vendor-work-order-offers.ts:10`, route `:17`), and `withdrawn` is a **manager** action (POST `:99-116` is manager-only). `vendorAgentRegistry` (`vendor-index.ts:23-45`) has `submit_bid` / `set_my_price` / `mark_job_done` — **no decline, no withdraw**.

A vendor who is booked, or does not cover that trade, has **no way to say no**. The offer sits in their list indefinitely and the manager waits for a response the product gives no way to send. Both sides are stuck, and the manager cannot tell "not interested" from "hasn't looked".

**Fix:** add `declined` with an optional reason, notify the manager, and let a vendor withdraw an unaccepted bid.

## 3. A vendor cannot withdraw, correct, or delete a submitted invoice

`/api/vendor/invoices` exposes only GET and POST (`route.ts:33, 53`). The only mutation is the **manager's** PATCH at `/api/vendor/invoices/[id]/decision`, and `vendor_invoices_vendor_read` makes the vendor SELECT-only at the database layer.

A vendor who fat-fingers a line item must ask the manager to reject it, then submit a duplicate. That is an awkward conversation the product forces on every typo.

**Fix:** allow edit/withdraw while the invoice is still `submitted` — the same compare-and-swap shape the bid path should use.

## 4. No export of invoices or payouts

There is no CSV or PDF export anywhere in the vendor portal. **A 1099 contractor needs this at year end** — it is the single most predictable request the portal will get every January, and today the answer is screenshotting a web page.

**Fix:** CSV export of payouts and invoices with a date range. PDF per invoice.

## Why grouped

Each is small; together they are the difference between a portal a contractor tolerates and one they trust. Split into separate tickets when scheduled — #1 is the only one that hard-blocks a real user today.
