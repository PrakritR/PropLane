## Description

Child of [PRP-260](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-260/epic-sms-first-operations-text-a-work-order-number-the-system-does-the). **Vendors are the users most likely to never open a portal** — they are on a roof, in a crawlspace, driving between jobs. If SMS-first works for anyone, it is them.

## Today

`vendorWorkOrderAgentRegistry` exists and is deliberately **answer-only**: reads pinned to one work order plus `escalate_to_manager`. A vendor can ask about a job over SMS but cannot *do* anything.

Meanwhile the portal itself blocks them in several ways ([PRP-254](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-254/vendor-gaps-multi-manager-vendors-cannot-invoice-cannot-decline-offers)): a vendor with two managers cannot invoice at all, and no vendor can decline an offer or correct an invoice anywhere.

## The loop to close

| Text | Action |
| -- | -- |
| `YES 1042` / `ACCEPT 1042` | accept the offer at the quoted price |
| `NO 1042` / `PASS` | **decline** — does not exist anywhere today ([PRP-254](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-254/vendor-gaps-multi-manager-vendors-cannot-invoice-cannot-decline-offers)) |
| `1042 $180` | submit or revise a bid |
| `1042 THU 2PM` | propose a visit window |
| `DONE 1042` | mark complete, trigger the resident's confirmation |
| `1042 INVOICE 180 parts 40 labor 140` | submit an invoice |
| `1042 PHOTO` + MMS | attach before/after evidence |

## The hard parts, honestly

* **Identity is weak.** The only credential is the `From` header. Everything above is a write, and some involve money. The existing rule — SMS gets the catalog **minus every** `destructive` **tool**, derived from the flag — must hold. An accept and an invoice are not destructive, but they *are* financial, so each needs an explicit confirm step ("Reply YES to confirm $180 invoice for WO-1042") rather than firing on first parse.
* **Free text is ambiguous.** `"1042 180"` — is that a price or a time? Parse conservatively and **ask** when unsure. A wrong invoice amount is worse than a clarifying question.
* **MMS costs more** and needs storage; treat photos as a later phase.
* **This depends on fixing the portal gaps first.** Building an SMS path to a feature that does not exist (decline, multi-manager invoicing) means building the feature twice. **Do** [PRP-254](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-254/vendor-gaps-multi-manager-vendors-cannot-invoice-cannot-decline-offers) **before this.**

## Why it is worth it

A vendor who can run their whole PropLane relationship from the messages app is a vendor who actually uses PropLane. Every other channel we offer them competes with a phone call to the manager — SMS is the only one that beats it.

Depends on [PRP-261](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-261/work-order-reference-resolution-make-andquotwo-1234andquot-a) (reference resolution), [PRP-262](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-262/work-order-event-orchestration-one-state-change-everyone-correctly) (orchestration), [PRP-254](https://linear.app/axishousing/issue/PRP-254/vendor-gaps-multi-manager-vendors-cannot-invoice-cannot-decline-offers) (the underlying capabilities).
